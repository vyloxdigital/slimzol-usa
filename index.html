The Slimzol formula, sorted into five groups

  • Four adaptogenic roots — maca, eleuthero, astragalus and Panax ginseng. The traditional-tonic end of the formula.

On the order of thingsBlend ingredients must be printed heaviest first. Maca opens the list and L-carnitine closes it, so there is more maca than carnitine in every dropperful. How much more, nobody outside the company can say.

None of these groups is a claim about what the product does. They are a way of seeing the shape of the formula: a broad, low-dose botanical blend with an amino acid tail and a single nutrient carrying an official percentage.

Group one

The four adaptogenic roots in Slimzol

Adaptogen is a herbalist's category rather than a pharmacological one, covering plants traditionally taken over long periods rather than for a specific complaint. Four of them open the Slimzol blend.

Maca rootLepidium meyenii

A cruciferous root grown on the Peruvian altiplano above four thousand metres, where it has been a food crop far longer than a supplement. First on the label, so the largest botanical share of the blend.

Eleuthero rootEleutherococcus senticosus

A thorny shrub from Siberia, Korea and northern China. A relative of ginseng rather than a true one, and the plant that gave the word adaptogen much of its early currency.

Astragalus rootAstragalus membranaceus

Known as huang qi and among the most used tonic roots in Chinese herbal practice, where it is traditionally simmered into broths rather than taken by the drop.

Panax ginseng rootPanax ginseng

True Asian ginseng, and the most heavily researched root in the family. Notably, it is the only botanical on the Slimzol label without an extract-ratio marker beside it.

Group two

The warming botanicals in Slimzol, and the caffeine question

Four plants in the blend are the kind that supplement formulators reach for when they want a formula to feel like it is doing something. Two of them bring caffeine.

Guarana seedPaullinia cupana

An Amazonian vine whose seed is richer in caffeine by weight than coffee. In Brazil it has been brewed and chewed for centuries before anyone put it in a dropper bottle.

Green tea leafCamellia sinensis

Unoxidised leaf, bringing catechins and a smaller amount of natural caffeine. The most studied plant on this page by a wide margin.

Cayenne pepper fruitCapsicum annuum

The chilli behind capsaicin. At blend-level quantities you will not taste heat, though a faint warmth after a dose would not be surprising.

Coleus forskohlii rootPlectranthus barbatus

An Indian member of the mint family, grown for the root compound forskolin and a long-standing fixture of this category of formula.

How much caffeine?The label does not say, and it does not have to, because none is added as an isolated ingredient. With the entire blend weighing 15.47 mg, the total has to be very small — but it is not nil, which is reason enough to take Slimzol in the morning.

This group is the practical argument for taking Slimzol early in the day rather than with an evening meal. If you are someone who feels a single afternoon espresso at bedtime, treat this formula the same way.

Group four

The amino acid side of Slimzol

After twelve plants, the Slimzol label changes register entirely. The last nine entries would look more at home on a tub of pre-workout powder than in a herbal shop, and they are listed here smallest-last, as the rules require.

  • Raspberry ketones. The aroma molecule that makes a raspberry smell like a raspberry. Produced industrially, since extracting it from fruit would be ruinously expensive.
  • L-glutamine. The most plentiful free amino acid in human blood, and a long-standing fixture of recovery formulas.
  • L-tyrosine. The raw material the body draws on to build dopamine, adrenaline and thyroid hormone.
  • L-arginine. Semi-essential, and the direct precursor the body uses to make nitric oxide.
  • Beta alanine. Best known from pre-workout drinks, where it feeds carnosine production in muscle tissue.
  • Monoammonium glycyrrhizinate. A liquorice root derivative, intensely sweet, and doing as much work as a sweetener here as anything else.
  • GABA. Gamma aminobutyric acid, the brain's main calming neurotransmitter, in supplemental form.
  • L-tryptophan. An essential amino acid you already get from ordinary protein, and the starting point for serotonin.
  • L-carnitine HCl. Final on the list, so the smallest share of all. Concentrated in muscle, and involved in moving fatty acids into cells.

Worth flaggingMonoammonium glycyrrhizinate comes from liquorice, one of the few botanicals clinicians routinely ask people with raised blood pressure to limit. The amount here is small. Mention it anyway if that applies to you.

Group five

Chromium, the one Slimzol ingredient with a Daily Value

Twenty-one ingredients sit inside a blend with a dagger beside it, meaning no official daily intake has been set for them. One ingredient sits above that line with a real number: chromium, at 7 mcg or 20% of the Daily Value.

It is an essential trace mineral, required in microgram quantities and not manufactured by the body. Broccoli, whole grains, potatoes, meat and brewer's yeast all contribute small amounts to an ordinary diet. The picolinate form pairs it with picolinic acid, a pairing supplement makers favour for its absorption. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements keeps a plain-English fact sheet on chromium covering intakes and the evidence so far.

A fifth of a day's intake is a top-up rather than an intervention. If you already take a multivitamin, check whether chromium is in that too, so you know what the two add up to.

The serving

What one dropperful of Slimzol actually measures

A dropperful is not a drop, and it is not the same volume on every brand's bottle. On this label it is defined for you: the serving size is one millilitre, and there are thirty servings in a 30 mL bottle. The arithmetic is unusually tidy.

In practice, a dropperful is what the pipette holds when you squeeze the bulb once and release it with the tip under the liquid. It will not fill the glass tube to the top, and it is not supposed to. The label's instruction is explicit about not exceeding the recommended dose, and a bottle that runs out early is the clearest sign of over-pouring.

A quick sanity checkIf your bottle is noticeably below half after fifteen days, you are drawing more than a millilitre. Thirty even servings is the design.

In practice

Fitting Slimzol into an ordinary day

The suggested use printed on the bottle is three sentences long: “Shake well before each use. Take 1 dropperful daily. Place in mouth and swallow.” Everything else is up to you, so here is what the rest of the label implies.

Morning, for the reason given above

Guarana and green tea put a little caffeine in every dose. Early in the day sidesteps the question entirely.

Attached to something you already do

Beside the kettle, next to a toothbrush, with the first glass of water. A supplement taken twenty mornings out of thirty has not failed on the formula; the routine simply had nowhere to attach itself.

Shaken properly, every single time

Botanical extracts settle in water. Without a proper shake, the first week of a bottle and the last week of it are not the same product.

Neat, or in a little water

The sweeteners and citrus flavouring exist so it can be taken straight. If you would rather not, dropping it into a small glass of water changes nothing about the dose.

A word of caution

Buying Slimzol without being caught out

Supplements sold direct rather than through shops attract imitators, and a liquid in a plain amber bottle is not hard to imitate badly. A few habits make that a non-issue.

  • Treat marketplace listings with suspicion. A third-party seller on a marketplace is not the manufacturer, and the guarantee on this page does not follow the product there.

Two minutes at the counter

Talking to a clinician about Slimzol

Dietary supplements are regulated as food rather than as medicine in the United States, which means nothing is reviewed before it goes on sale and no pharmacist has been handed a monograph for this product. The FDA explains that framework on its dietary supplements pages. It is also exactly why a photograph of the panel is worth more than a web page.

What to show them

Photograph the Supplement Facts panel on your bottle and show it alongside your current prescriptions. That single image answers more than any description could.

The entries most likely to prompt a question

  • Green tea extract, which comes up most often alongside anticoagulants.
  • Panax ginseng, which carries a long list of theoretical interactions including with diabetes medicines.
  • Guarana, because it is caffeine, and caffeine interacts more widely than people expect.
  • Monoammonium glycyrrhizinate, the liquorice derivative, relevant to blood pressure and potassium.
  • Chromium and Gymnema, both worth raising with anyone managing blood sugar.

None of this implies the product is unsafe. It means one two-minute conversation can answer what no article is in a position to.

Slimzol questions and answers

What is Slimzol?

Slimzol is a dietary supplement that happens to be a liquid rather than a capsule, marketed as weight loss support. The bottle holds 1 Fl Oz, or 30 mL, with a dropper cap and thirty servings of one millilitre each, which works out at a month per bottle. Each serving provides 7 mcg of chromium as chromium picolinate, equal to 20% of the Daily Value, plus a 15.47 mg proprietary blend of twenty-one botanical extracts, amino acids and related compounds.

What is the full Slimzol ingredient list?

Chromium picolinate, followed by a blend of maca root, African mango seed, grape seed, guarana seed, eleuthero root, astragalus root, green tea leaf, Gymnema sylvestre leaf, Coleus forskohlii root, cayenne pepper fruit, grapefruit seed, Panax ginseng root, raspberry ketones, L-glutamine, L-tyrosine, L-arginine, beta alanine, monoammonium glycyrrhizinate, GABA, L-tryptophan and L-carnitine HCl. How do you take Slimzol?

The bottle says to shake well before each use, take one dropperful daily, place it in the mouth and swallow, and not to exceed that dose. A dropperful is 1 mL. Because guarana and green tea each contribute a little natural caffeine, most people find the morning suits it better than the evening.

How many servings are in a bottle of Slimzol?

Thirty. The panel states a serving size of 1 ml and thirty servings per container, so a single bottle lasts a month. That makes two bottles sixty servings, three bottles ninety and six bottles one hundred and eighty.

Does Slimzol contain caffeine or sugar?

No caffeine is added as an isolated ingredient, though guarana seed and green tea leaf both contain some naturally; with the whole blend weighing 15.47 mg the quantity is necessarily small. There is no sugar: the panel records zero calories and lists the product as not a significant source of total or added sugars, with sweetness coming from stevia, xylitol and a liquorice derivative.

What does Slimzol cost per day?

Is Slimzol sold on Amazon or in stores?

No. It is sold through the company's own checkout, which is where the order buttons on this page lead. Pharmacies and supermarkets do not stock it, and a marketplace listing is a third-party seller rather than the manufacturer, so the guarantee described here would not necessarily apply.

Who should not take Slimzol?

The label excludes anyone under 18 and anyone pregnant or nursing, and instructs anyone with a known medical condition to consult a physician first. It also says to keep the product out of the reach of children and not to use a bottle whose safety seal is damaged or missing. A short Slimzol glossary

Label language, translated.

Dropperful
What the pipette holds after one squeeze and release. Defined on this label as 1 mL, with thirty per bottle. The word means different volumes on different brands.
Proprietary blend
Several ingredients given one combined weight rather than individual amounts. Entirely legal, very common, and the reason the exact quantity of any single botanical is not public.
20:1 extract equivalent
Twenty parts of dried plant reduced to one part of extract. The caret beside eleven of the twelve botanicals on this label.
%Daily Value
The share of a day's recommended intake one serving supplies, calculated against a 2,000 calorie diet. Slimzol's chromium is 20%.
Daily Value not established
What the dagger beside the blend signifies: no official recommended intake has been set for those twenty-one ingredients. It says nothing about whether they are regulated.
Other Ingredients
Everything present that is not an active: here the water base, preservative, flavouring and sweeteners.
Natural flavors
A regulated term covering flavouring from plant or animal origin, without specifying which.
Dietary supplement
A category that sits under food law rather than drug law. Products in it reach the shelf without pre-approval, and none may be advertised as treating a disease.
